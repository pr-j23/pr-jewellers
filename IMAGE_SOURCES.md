## Recommended Image Sources

- **Unsplash** — High-quality photography with permissive licensing. Download fixed-resolution images for local bundling; attribution is optional but encouraged.
- **Pexels** — Extensive catalog of lifestyle and product shots under a free-to-use license. Ideal for consistent branding assets.
- **Pixabay** — Offers photos, vectors, and illustrations with minimal restrictions, making it flexible for assorted design needs.
- **Burst (by Shopify)** — Curated collections tailored for retail and ecommerce imagery, good for product-style visuals.
